# make me  Breakfast

## step 1

Goto the kitchen, locate the door beside the wine bar and open the door that's the kitchen door

## step 2

Open the kitchen drawer  close to the water dispenser collect 2(two) plates from the set of 10(ten) plate  and a fork from the set of 12(twelve) fork, goto the sink open the tap by turning the hand anti-close wise and rinse the plates for 70 seconds then lock the tap by turning the hand clock wise after rinsing the plate

## step 3

Go to the kitchen table opposite the chiller and place one plate with the fork on the table

## step 4

Go to  the chiller with the other plate, open the chiller door check for the egg rack inside the chiller, collect 4(four) eggs out of the 28(twenty-eight) eggs and place it inside the plate  and close the chiller door

## step 5

Go back to the table where you place the other plate and fork , use the fork to crack the egg one after the other inside the plate on the table and check if any shell is inside the mxture no shell inside move to the next task but if there is a shell remove it by dipping the fork inside the egg mixture and pick it out 

## step 6

locate the trash can  close to the exist door and drop the egg shell inside the trash can

## step 7

Go to the kitchen drawer on the right side of the microwave, open the drawer and collect the can of salt and seasons then close the drawer

## step 8

Goto the drawer close to the chiller and collect a knife and a chop board and go back to the table where you place the cracked eggs, place it beside the plate containing the cracked eggs 

## step 9

Go back to the chiller, open the chiller door collect 4(four)pepper from the 50 pepper, 2(two) tomatoes from the 10(ten) tomatoes and close the chiller door , then check the basket close to the kitchen storage room  entrance  door and collect 1(one) onion and go back to the  table

## step 10 

place the pepper,tomatoes and onion on the table 

## step 11

place the pepper on the chop board and used the knife to slice it into tiny fine slice of 0.2cm each, after that do same with the tomatoes and onions 

## step 12 

Go to the utensil drawer on the left side of the microwave, open the drawer collect a frying pan from the set of 4 fry pan,  a flat head frying spoon from the 3 set of spoon and close the drawer and goto the sink and open the tap by turning the hand close-wise  rinse the utensils for 120 seconds then close  the tap by turing the hand anti-clockwise after rinsing the utensils(the frying pan and frying spoon ) 

## step 13

Go to the gas cooker turn it on, after ignition  reduce the heat put it at average ensure is on , place the pan on the gas cooker collect the can of oil from the drawer beside the gas cooker and pour 5ml oil of  on the pan allow it heat for 5(five) minutes  go back to the table where the egg was placed and apply a pinch of salt inside the cracked eggs and stir to form a mixture then collect the sliced tomatoes, pepper and onions, go back to the pan on heat on the gas cooker and pour the sliced tomatoes, pepper and onions inside it  after that stir it and allow it to heat for 6(six) minutes .  Go back to the table and collect the stirred eggs and pour it inside the pan containing the tomatoes, pepper and onions on heat and stir everything together allow it to heat for 4 minutes(four minutes)  and turn off the gas cooker ensure the gas cooker is off

## step 14

Go to the drawer close to the window and open the drawer collect a flat plate and a teaspoon  close the drawer then go to the chiller open the chiller collect 8 slices of bread from the pack of bread and a yellow Can of butter close to the bottle of 50cl coke , place the  bread inside the plate , move to the table place the plate on the table and open the Can of butter and use the teaspoon to collect one teaspoon of butter and apply the butter on the bread one after the other by dipping the teaspoon inside the butter ensure the eight slice of bread contains butter. Rinse the plate use for stirring the egg for 2(two) minutes with water and wash with a sponge by applying a dish washing mixture, ensure is clean and well rinsed ,allow to dry and do same for the  chop board use for cutting. After that go back to the gas cooker and pour the fried eggs inside the plate , use the rinse plate that was use to stirring  the egg to collect the fried eggs and place the plate beside the bread on the table. Return the chop board, knife and frying pan and spoon back to their respective drawers that they where collected from and ensure it is well placed before closing the drawer

## step 15 

Go to the fridge and collect a 75cl bottle  water then place it beside the bread and egg on the table get a tray from the tray stand and place the bread, fried eggs and bottle water on the tray. Food is ready to serve

## step 16

Take the tray and place it on the dinning table.And ensure the food is well served
